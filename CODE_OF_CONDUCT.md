# Code of Conduct

PRsight follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This page summarises it; the full text applies.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Respect different opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, or sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests and discussions, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer privately. Use the contact details on the maintainer's GitHub profile, or open a [private security advisory](../../security/advisories/new) if no other channel suits. All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code. In order of severity, the responses are:

1. a private warning
2. a temporary ban
3. a permanent ban

See the [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) for details.
